# TBEA HQ Meeting Brief — Ulusoy Delegation

**Internal. Ulusoy only. Do not share with TBEA.**
Meeting: TBEA Headquarters, China · November 2026
Deck: Ulusoy - TBEA Partnership (57 slides; there is a 40-minute short path in the speaker notes)

---

## 1. Meeting objective

By the end of the meeting, TBEA management should conclude:

> **"Ulusoy could become a serious long-term strategic market-development partner for TBEA in Türkiye and the surrounding region."**

**A good outcome, in order of importance:**
1. A named TBEA team (and ideally a senior sponsor) for the partnership.
2. A positive first discussion of project and account registration (the principle, not terms).
3. A date for a working session within 30 days.
4. Agreement to start technical review of Karnobat and Radilovo/Katselovo.

**A great outcome:** TBEA names priority product families for Türkiye and commits to sending qualification and type-test packages.

**An acceptable minimum:** a contact person and a follow-up date. Do not leave without at least this.

---

## 2. Five messages TBEA must remember

1. **Ulusoy is an 89-year family enterprise, not a trading start-up.** It has three generations, ports, fleets, energy assets and decades-long partnerships with Volvo and Neoplan, and now a Chinese manufacturer (Lonyu).
2. **Türkiye is in a structural grid investment cycle.** Demand is set to rise to 680 TWh by 2040. The plan calls for 120 GW of wind and solar by 2035 and $28bn of transmission investment, against a 33 GW storage pipeline with only about 0.2 GW installed.
3. **Not every door is open, and we know which ones are.** The open doors are private 154 kV connection substations, World Bank/IFI transmission packages, data centers and selected BESS. Public tenders with domestic preference and commodity equipment come later.
4. **We already have real projects.** Karnobat (built and expanding), Radilovo + Katselovo (371 MW PV, 1,200 MWh BESS), PLAKE + Golem Kamen (140 MW wind) and a Middle East off-grid system.
5. **The model is staged and protected.** It runs from market development to execution to localization, each stage earned by results. We ask for registration of what we originate, not blanket exclusivity.

---

## 3. Five things we want from TBEA

1. **People:** a responsible team, a senior sponsor and a single point of contact.
2. **Protection:** registration and protection of the projects and accounts we originate, agreed in principle now.
3. **Focus:** priority product families for Türkiye, ideally 154 kV power transformers, 170 kV GIS and CT/VT, 36 kV switchgear, PCS/BESS and protection and automation.
4. **Proof:** qualification packages (type tests, IEC certificates, references, especially World Bank/ADB-financed and European ones).
5. **Access:** direct coordination with the relevant factories and engineering teams, plus a working-session date.

(Financing support and a joint pipeline review are also on the slide. Treat them as secondary today.)

---

## 4. Sensitive topics to avoid

**Never mention:**
- "ChinaWorks" or any internal platform name. Use Ulusoy Group, Ulusoy Energy or Ulusoy Robotics.
- CRRC, Orient or any other Chinese partner beyond Lonyu, which is already public in our corporate deck. Do not suggest TBEA is one of several OEMs in a portfolio.
- Commission rates, fee expectations, margins or target revenue for Ulusoy.
- Internal probability scoring, the pipeline dashboard, or how we rank opportunities.
- Names of individual decision-makers, the strength of our relationships with them, or the customer database.
- Competitor pricing or weaknesses.
- Project-level confidential details: Karnobat company registration and permit numbers, the developer's financial-model figures (IRR, debt amounts, covenants), or draft contract terms for Radilovo/Katselovo.
- The name of the Middle East country or client unless we have explicit permission.
- The developer partner's company name (the decks are branded "Tanay Energy"), unless you have agreed in advance to disclose it.

**Avoid claiming:**
- That TBEA is selected, approved or ordered on any project.
- That capacities are final (they are working values).
- That Siemens Energy has a Turkish transformer/GIS factory (unconfirmed). Say "Siemens: LV/MV switchgear in Gebze".
- Any precise current data-center MW figure (estimates conflict). Say "about 250 MW".

**Handle carefully:**
- The current status of the Çeşme Port / Trieste corridor and the Volvo relationship. Confirm the facts internally before the meeting. Present them as operating history if they are no longer active.
- Questions about why we are not already working with another manufacturer. Answer: "We chose to approach TBEA first because of the portfolio fit."

## 5. Likely objections

1. "We already have an overseas sales organisation / Turkish agent."
2. "Give us your customer list first; then we can decide."
3. "We cannot agree to protection or exclusivity at this stage."
4. "Türkiye has domestic preference. Why would we win?"
5. "Your projects are small or early-stage."
6. "You have little track record in high-voltage equipment."
7. "Why not just bid World Bank tenders ourselves?"
8. "We would rather set up our own subsidiary or factory in Türkiye."
9. "What is your commercial model or commission?"
10. "Bulgaria and North Macedonia are EU or EU-candidate markets. Our European team covers them."

## 6. Suggested responses

1. **Existing organisation:** "We do not replace your international team. We give it a local market-development arm with senior access to developers, EPCs and industrial groups that a regional office cannot easily build. If an agent exists, we can define clear segments and accounts so there is no conflict."
2. **Customer list:** "We will be fully transparent inside the registration framework. Agreeing the framework first protects both sides. In the working session we go through the pipeline account by account with your team."
3. **Protection:** "We are not asking for exclusivity. We ask only that what we originate and register is protected for an agreed, activity-based period. It costs TBEA nothing and gives you one clean channel per account."
4. **Domestic preference:** "Exactly why we do not start with public tenders. Private connection substations, data centers and BESS have no domestic preference. In World Bank contracts it applies only if the bidding documents include it. Later, local assembly could unlock even the public segment."
5. **Projects:** "Karnobat is built and operating, with substations and a 110 kV line in service. Radilovo/Katselovo is about 1.2 GWh of storage. These are the first doors, not the whole pipeline. Türkiye is the bigger prize."
6. **HV track record:** "Correct, and that is the point. TBEA brings the HV technology. We bring the market. We have owned grid-connected generation, represented international brands for decades, and run complex infrastructure. Our technical-sales capability grows with the pipeline."
7. **World Bank alone:** "You can see the published tender. A local partner adds earlier knowledge of package scope, a Turkish EPC consortium, an understanding of evaluation, and site, logistics and after-sales commitments."
8. **Own subsidiary:** "That is a valid long-term option and fits our Stage 3. Doing it before demand is proven is expensive. We can be the bridge to that point, and a partner in it if it makes sense."
9. **Commercial model:** "We will propose a clear structure aligned with TBEA's partner policies in the working session. Today we want to agree roles and principles."
10. **EU markets:** "We bring specific projects through our partners in those countries. We are happy to coordinate with your European team project by project. Registration avoids any overlap."

## 7. Questions we should ask TBEA

1. Which TBEA business unit or company would own Türkiye: TBEA Electrical Equipment Group's international arm, or the product companies directly?
2. What is TBEA's current presence in Türkiye: sales, agents, completed projects, any TEİAŞ-approved equipment?
3. Which product families does TBEA itself see as priorities for Türkiye and the region?
4. Do you have 170 kV-class GIS, CT/VT and 154 kV transformer designs with IEC type tests? Have any been supplied to Türkiye or to 154 kV systems elsewhere?
5. Which references could be shown to Turkish buyers: World Bank/ADB-financed, European, Middle East?
6. How does TBEA normally structure regional market-development partners: registration, protection periods, conflict rules?
7. Can TBEA's battery container and PCS be certified for 55 °C ambient, as the Middle East project needs?
8. Is TBEA interested in the HVDC programme in Türkiye, and has it been in contact with TEİAŞ or its consultants?
9. What financing support can TBEA offer for projects (export credit, Sinosure, supplier credit), and on what minimum size?
10. What are typical delivery times today for 154 kV-class power transformers and GIS, and is there capacity for Türkiye?

---

## 8. Negotiation points NOT to settle in the first meeting

- Commission or fee levels, payment timing and structure.
- Length of protection periods and renewal criteria (agree the principle only).
- Any exclusivity: territorial, product or segment.
- Who carries contract and commercial risk (direct TBEA supply vs. supply through a Turkish EPC vs. Ulusoy as contracting party).
- Responsibility and cost-sharing for local service, spares and warehousing.
- Any joint venture, investment or localization terms.
- Pricing of specific projects (Karnobat, Radilovo/Katselovo).
- Use of the TBEA name or logo in Ulusoy marketing. Agree this later, in writing.
- Non-compete terms that could restrict Ulusoy's other businesses (for example robotics).

If TBEA pushes on any of these, say: *"That is an important point. We suggest we prepare a proposal for the working session so both sides can review it properly."*

## 9. Recommended meeting closing

After slide 56 (proposed next steps), pause and let TBEA respond. Do not push for decisions in the room. Then say:

> "Thank you for the discussion. If TBEA sees value in continuing, perhaps we could agree on a contact person, so we can send the materials and propose dates for a working session."

Then move to slide 57 (thank you) and finish. Let the most senior TBEA person respond first. Before leaving:
- Confirm names, titles and contact details of the TBEA team. Exchange business cards with both hands.
- Confirm the follow-up date, or at least the week.
- Agree who sends the meeting summary (offer that Ulusoy does, within 48 hours).
- If appropriate, invite TBEA to visit Türkiye, including the Ankara data-center area and a customer or EPC meeting.
