> **Two things the deck must get right**
> 1. **Two different capacity series.** The Ministry/TEİAŞ series counts **electrical MW (MWe)**: 14,774 MW at end-2025 and 15,540 MW at end-Sep 2026. TÜREB counts **mechanical MW (MWm)**: 15,934 MW at end-2025. Don't mix them on one slide.
> 2. **"Ulusoy" is ambiguous.** *Ulusoy Elektrik* (BIST: ULUSE, medium-voltage switchgear) has been **82.275% owned by Eaton since 15 Apr 2019**; the Ulusoy family sold that stake. *Ulusoy Un* (BIST: ULUUN, flour) is a separate listed company with small wind and solar self-supply plants. The deck should say exactly which Ulusoy entity is the partner (see §9).

## 2. YEKA RES auctions and local content

**YEKA RES-2024** (held about Dec 2024 / early 2025): 1,200 MW in five zones across Edirne, Kırklareli and Sivas, all cleared at the 3.5 USc/kWh floor. **Enerjisa Üretim won 2 of 5**, including Edirne (US$60k/MW contribution). 49-year connection; 6 years on the free market with a floor; US$50k/MW bond. H** for 1.2 GW. "2,000 MW" is the RES-2024 and GES-2024 rounds combined.

**YEKA RES-2025** (held 9 Dec 2025): **1,150 MW** in 6 zones (Balıkesir 1/2/3, Aydın-Denizli, Kütahya, Sivas 500 MW). All cleared at the **€35/MWh floor** (ceiling €55). All winners are Turkish. Known winners: Balıkesir-3 (110 MW) Eksim (€312k/MW); Balıkesir-2 (120 MW) Balıkesir Elektrik (€218k/MW); Kütahya, likely Enerjisa (truncated extract). Revenue to the state: about €208m. H** (totals) / **M** (winner list incomplete). Nordex later booked RES-2025 turbine orders: Efor 120 MW (Balıkesir-2) and Eksim 110 MW (Balıkesir-3).

*Sales angle:* these **seven 2026 winners (Egesa, FE Girişim, Metgün, Ecogreen, Poyraz)** have not yet announced turbine orders in the search results. They are the near-term buyers for 1.5 GW. Projects must meet YEKA local content, so turbines must be built in Türkiye.

**Local-content rules in YEKA wind (key barrier and opportunity):**
- **Points system.** YEKA RES-2025 and RES-2026 specs require turbines to earn **at least 55 "yerlilik" points**. Points are summed over components listed in Annex-2. A component scores its full points **only with a Yerli Malı Belgesi** (domestic goods certificate) proving its minimum domestic ratio; a component without the certificate scores zero. **This is 55 points, not 55% local content.** Annex-2 items extracted include nacelle cover (2 pts), nacelle frame (3), gearbox (5), turbine transformer (1.5) and internal crane (0.5). The RES-2026 annex now has separate rows for **electromagnetic direct-drive (B.1)** and **permanent-magnet direct-drive (B.2)** generators. Sources: RES-2026 Şartname PDF (fetch blocked) – https://enerji.gov.tr/Media/Dizin/BHIM/tr/Duyurular/YEKA_RES-2026Sartname_202607111136.pdf ; Yeşil Ekonomi – https://yesilekonomi.com/ruzgar-yeka-yarismasi-sartlari-guncellendi/ ; Paksoy – https://paksoy.av.tr/2026/08/ges-ve-res-yeka-2026-ihaleleri/ . **Conf. M-H.**
- **Component minimum ratios** (Oct-2025 amendment to the RES-2025 spec): **tower 65%, tower connection elements 70%, blades 65%, all other components 51%**. Source: paradergi – https://www.paradergi.com.tr/sektorler/2025/11/24/14-yarisma-icin-gozler-enerji-bakanliginda . **Conf. M.** One extract says that "tower 65 / blade 60 / other 51" belongs to an older round; check the Annex in the 2026 spec.
- **Incentive regime (separate from YEKA).** Reported 2025 changes require domestic **blades and towers** for wind investment incentives, and from **1 Jan 2026** also a domestically made **nacelle and generator**. Projects that already held a YEKA contract or pre-licence are exempt. Sources: donanimhaber – https://www.donanimhaber.com/ges-res-ve-sarj-istasyonlarinda-yerli-uretim-zorunlulugu-artti--192954 ; Enerji Günlüğü – https://www.enerjigunlugu.net/ges-ve-resde-yerli-uretim-sarti-artiyor-63783h.htm . **Conf. M.** The primary communiqué was not retrieved.
- **Factory obligation.** The early YEKA RES-2017 (1 GW, Siemens-Türkerler) **required a domestic nacelle factory and R&D**. Later rounds dropped this. **No 2024–2026 onshore round requires building a factory**; the obligation is now delivered through the points and certificate system. Conf. M, from background knowledge; not re-verified in this session.
- **Offshore:** 25% of total capex must be domestic (see §1).
- **Localisation level today:** the Minister says turbine localisation is about 60%, and above 70% for towers, generators and blades (Daily Sabah). **Conf. M.**

**YEKDEM local-content bonus history:**
- 2011–2020 (Law 5346, USD-based): wind FiT 7.3 USc/kWh for 10 years, plus a domestic-component bonus of up to **+3.7 USc/kWh for 5 years** (blade, generator, tower, etc.). Conf. M (background; the Mondaq search extract lists 7.30 USc for wind).
- From 1 Jul 2021 (Presidential Decision 3453, TL-based after Law 7257 amended Art. 6/B): wind 32 kr/kWh for 10 years, plus a local-content bonus of **8 kr/kWh (0.08 TL/kWh) for 5 years**, with USD caps. **Note: the "0.8 USc/kWh" figure circulating is not verified. The correct figure is 0.08 TL/kWh.** Sources: CMS – https://cms.law/en/tur/legal-updates/turkey-introduces-new-feed-in-tariff-for-renewable-energy-support-mechanism ; Mondaq – https://www.mondaq.com/turkey/renewables/1013678/changes-to-renewable-energy-support-mechanism . **Conf. H.**
- YEKA contracts now use their own price (USD or EUR cents) and do not use the YEKDEM bonus.

## 3. Other routes to market in Türkiye

- **Capacity increase ("kapasite artışı"):** legally a licence amendment. The Oct-2023 amendment to the Electricity Market Licence Regulation ties wind/solar grid allocation to capacity-increase requests from existing licence holders. Added **mechanical** MW cannot exceed the licensed **electrical** MW (including what is already operating). A new EIA (ÇED) is usually needed, and a RİTM wind-forecast connection is required (2024 regulation). Sources: alomaliye (14 Oct 2023 RG) – https://www.alomaliye.com/2023/10/15/elektrik-piyasasi-lisans-yonetmeliginde-degisiklik-14-10-2023/ ; FFK law firm – https://www.ffkpartnerhukuk.com.tr/res-kapasite-artisi-hukuki-sureci-2026-guncel-rehber/ . **Conf. M.** **I could not find a published total for the 2023–2026 surge in capacity-increase applications.** Market evidence: Akfen ordered 102 MW from Nordex (2024) to extend existing farms – https://www.dailysabah.com/business/energy/turkiyes-akfen-renewable-to-expand-wind-farms-under-nordex-deal ; many 2026 Nordex orders (389 MW in Oct 2026) are not tied to YEKA.
- **Storage-integrated wind (depolamalı RES):** after the Nov-2022 rules, more than **252 GW** of applications came in within 6 months. EPDK has granted pre-licences for **33.1 GW** of storage-integrated projects, of which **263 are wind (RES)**; an earlier tally was 13,529 MW for 193 RES projects. New applications have been suspended since **Oct 2023**. Only **208 MW** of storage-integrated wind and solar was commissioned by Feb 2026 (Ember). The Minister cites 33.5 GW held by investors, with about 2 GW expected online in 2026. Sources: Türkgün – https://www.turkgun.com/ekonomi/epdkdan-buyuk-atilim-676-projeye-toplam-331-gigavat-on-lisans-verildi/278602 ; Ember – https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/turkiye-electricity-review-2026/policy-recommendations/ ; Ekonomi Gazetesi (5 licences cancelled for delays) – https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/sektor-haberleri/depolamali-yatirimlari-geciktiren-5-sirketin-proje-ruhsatlari-iptal-edildi-67026 . **Conf. H.** This is a large, delayed pipeline of wind plus BESS. It is a natural fit for CRRC's combined turbine, PCS and BESS offer. Example: Kontrolmatik with **Harbin Electric** on a 1 GWh wind-plus-storage project – https://www.energy-storage.news/kontrolmatik-and-chinas-harbin-electric-to-deploy-first-1gwh-wind-plus-storage-project-in-turkey .
- **Hybrid plants (wind host plus solar auxiliary):** about **1.4 GW** of hybrid solar was installed by May 2025, about two-thirds of it at **wind** hosts. The 100 MW hybrid cap was lifted for wind in Oct 2022. Ember sees at least **8 GW** of hybrid solar potential at existing wind and hydro plants. Source: Ember – https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/turkiye-can-bypass-grid-constraints-with-hybrid-solar-power-plants/status-of-hybrid-solar-plants-in-turkiye . **Conf. H.** This is the inverter angle for CRRC.
- **Repowering and fleet age:** commercial wind began in 1998–99. A 2025 TÜREB/TWEA survey counts **472 wind farms and more than 5,000 turbines**, and **29.6% of capacity is turbines below 3 MW**. The O&M market is about **US$350m**. Example: Alaçatı RES is replacing 12 old turbines with 2 domestic ones (Jan 2026). **No dedicated repowering regulation was found.** Sources: AA – https://www.aa.com.tr/en/energy/wind/turkish-wind-sectors-operation-maintenance-market-reaches-350m/60741 ; alomaliye – https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/01/13/ruzgar-turbinlerinde-yerlilik-hamlesi/ . **Conf. M.**

## 5. Trade measures

- **Türkiye anti-dumping on Chinese wind blades:** opened **28 Jun 2025** (Communiqué 2025/14) after a domestic producer's complaint. **Terminated without duties on 31 Dec 2025** because the complainant stopped cooperating. ## Best 10 facts for slides
1. **Türkiye has 15,540 MW of wind (12.2% of 127.4 GW)** as of end-Sep 2026, up 766 MW year to date. *(Energy Ministry via Daily Sabah, H)*
2. **2025 was a record year: about 2.1 GW installed**, making Türkiye **#2 in Europe** for new wind. The industry expects about 2.5 GW in 2026, which the government calls the "year of wind". *(TÜREB/GWEC/AA, H)*
3. **The National Energy 2040 plan (5 Nov 2026) takes wind from 15 GW to 63 GW** (about 57 GW onshore and 6 GW offshore), within 175 GW of solar plus wind and about US$100bn of investment. That means roughly 3 GW of wind a year for 15 years. *(AA/Daily Sabah/Marinelink, H/M)*
4. **The China–Türkiye MoU (12 Nov 2026, COP31)** covers up to **US$15bn for 7 GW** of wind and solar plus storage, explicitly tied to **local turbine manufacturing and technology transfer**. It names no Chinese company yet, so the window is open now. *(Daily Sabah/Euronews, H)*
5. **YEKA RES-2026: 1,500 MWe in 7 zones to 5 Turkish developers** (Egesa, FE Girişim, Metgün, Ecogreen, Poyraz) at 3.50 €c/kWh, with €62.9m of fees. None has announced a turbine order yet. *(AA/Enerdata, H)*
6. **Local content is the gate.** YEKA turbines need **at least 55 "yerlilik" points backed by Yerli Malı certificates** (tower 65%, blades 65%, other parts 51% minimum ratios). From 2026, incentives also require a domestic nacelle and generator. **Offshore: 25% of capex, US$5m penalty per point.** *(YEKA specs/press, M-H)*
7. **Incumbents are localising fast.** Nordex (34% share) opened a 1,200 blades/yr plant in İzmir in May 2026 and signed a record 525 MW order. Enercon (24%) is building its own Bergama blade plant, with generators and towers from ATEŞ. *(Nordex/Enercon, H)*
8. **Chinese OEMs have a foothold but no factory yet.** Goldwind (about 2% share, more than 1.2 GW installed plus contracted, blade plant still at site selection), Envision (232 MW Karaman) and Dongfang (US$250m, 2 GW/yr plant still a proposal). **A Turkish anti-dumping probe on Chinese blades closed without duties on 31 Dec 2025.** *(H/M)*
9. **There is a 33 GW storage-integrated pre-licence backlog** (263 wind projects), with only about 208 MW built. Plus about 1.4 GW of hybrid solar, two-thirds of it at wind sites. This suits CRRC's combined turbine, PCS and BESS offer. *(EPDK/Ember, H)*
10. **CRRC brings proven Turkish localisation and global scale.** It is **#7 global wind OEM in 2025** (GWEC), with a **TÜV SÜD-certified 7.15 MW D195** and a **6.25 MW D175 EU platform** (first unit June 2026). It has delivered **more than 400 metro cars localised through the CRRC-MNG plant in Ankara** (M11: 60% local), and is partnered with Limak and MAPA on the **AED 20.5bn Dubai Blue Line**. In the EU, the **FSR probe of Goldwind** (in-depth since Feb 2026) makes Serbia and North Macedonia, outside the EU, plus Türkiye the lower-friction route. SANY (Serbia, 168 MW) and Envision (North Macedonia, Štip 131 MW) have already shown this works. *(H)*
