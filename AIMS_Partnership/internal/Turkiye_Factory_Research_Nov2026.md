# Türkiye: Towards Modern, Automated Factories — Research Notes for the AIMS Technology Meeting

Prepared 20 Nov 2026 for Ulusoy Robotics (distributor of Lonyu heavy-load AGVs).

**How this was researched.** The facts come from web search result excerpts and from Bigdata.com news and document chunks (https://bigdata.com). Direct page fetching was blocked in this session for most news domains, including ekonomim.com and transformer-technology.com. So most items were checked against the search excerpt or indexed article text, not the full page. Click through to the source before you put a number on a slide.

**Confidence levels:**
- **H**: a primary source (company or government) or several consistent reputable outlets.
- **M**: a single reputable secondary source, a trade-data aggregator, or sources that differ slightly.
- **L**: a weak or unclear source, or one with conflicting dates.

## Best 12 facts for slides

1. **Transformer export growth:** Türkiye's exports of transformers, converters and inductors (HS 8504) rose 12.9% to about US$2.7bn in 2025, up from US$1.5bn in 2022. Source: https://corplox.net/trade/tr/hs/85/8504 (M)
2. **Astor capacity tripling:** Astor Enerji is investing US$150m in Ankara (Sincan OSB) to lift power transformer capacity from 32,000 to 100,000 MVA a year, with the new capacity coming on stream in early 2027. Source: https://www.capital.com.tr/sektorler/enerji/astor-enerjiden-150-milyon-dolarlik-dev-yatirim (H)
3. **Export pull for Astor:** In March 2026 Astor signed US$768.9m of power transformer orders with US utilities, delivering through 2028. Source: https://kap.org.tr/en/Bildirim/1575359 (H)
4. **Hitachi Energy:** Hitachi Energy put US$70m into a new low-carbon power transformer factory in Dilovası (Kocaeli), opened June 2026, raising capacity by 70%. Source: https://www.hitachienergy.com/news-and-events/features/2026/06/hitachi-energy-inaugurates-power-transformers-factory-and-service-center-in-dilovas-turkiye (H)
5. **More new transformer plants:** Europower (US$100m, Ankara, producing since 2025), Beta Enerji (US$130m campus, Adana) and EKOS (US$50m, Balıkesir) have all built or are building new transformer plants. Sources: https://www.borsagundem.com.tr/europower-enerji-100-milyon-dolarlik-guc-trafosu-fabrikasini-tamamladi ; https://www.adanahaber.net/haber/beta-energy-commissioned-the-first-phase-of-its-new-130-million-campus-35390.html ; https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/enerjiterminali/finans/ekos-elektrik-50-milyon-dolarlik-yatirimla-balikesirde-yeni-fabrikanin-temelini-atti/49366 (H/M)
6. **Battery pipeline:** Türkiye's 33 GW battery-storage pipeline is larger than any EU country's, and it is feeding local pack and cell plants such as LGES–GO Enerji in Ankara (2.5 → 7.5 GWh) and Pomega (3 GWh). Sources: https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/turkiye-electricity-review-2026/storage ; https://www.electrive.com/2025/12/02/lg-energy-solution-and-go-enerji-to-build-battery-factory-in-ankara (H)
