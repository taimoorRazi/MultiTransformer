# AIMS Visit — Meeting Notes (23 November 2026)

**Classification:** Ulusoy internal.

## 1. How AIMS is organised
AIMS works with two main teams on every project. The **solution department** analyses the customer, designs the concept and prepares the offer. The **implementation department** takes over after the contract and manages installation, commissioning and handover. AIMS also has a planning department working on software for the steel industry.

## 2. Process-optimisation software for furnaces
AIMS has about eight years of experience with furnace (oven) management software, mainly in steel. It has no international project in this area yet. The software works on both **electric furnaces** and **reheating furnaces**.
- **Reheating furnaces:** AIMS claims **20–30% energy savings**.
- **Electric furnaces:** easier to implement, but the savings are smaller because these plants usually already have good control systems.

The term "soup cooking" in the original notes is unclear; it may refer to soaking furnaces, so confirm with AIMS.

**How it works:** the process-management software sits on top of the plant's existing control system. The customer's PLC controls the heaters, valves and blowers, and therefore the temperature. AIMS's software talks to that PLC and the SCADA system and optimises the settings. It does not replace them.

*Note:* AIMS's brochure shows 2–5% gas savings on steel furnaces. Ask for a real reference with before-and-after data before quoting 20–30% to any customer.

## 3. How a project is sold and designed
1. **First contact:** the AIMS sales person and a solution engineer hold an online meeting with the customer to collect information. Then they visit the site.
2. **Concept:** based on the customer's data, the solution team prepares a first concept: layout drawings and calculations. A simple task takes about **2 weeks**, a complex one about **1 month**. This depends on AIMS's engineers having free access to the customer's site and information.
3. **Iterations:** the concept is presented in PowerPoint, with the drawings, calculations and figures the investor needs. **The feasibility study and concept are free of charge.** Concept meetings repeat several times, because new requirements and ideas come up after each one. This typically takes **1–2 months**.
4. **Approval:** the customer approves the final concept with a signature.
5. **Offer:** AIMS then prepares the **bill of quantities (BOQ), final price and commercial agreement within about 2 days**.

**Greenfield vs brownfield:**
- **Greenfield (new factory):** AIMS must work with the building contractor. The concept must fit the hall and workshop dimensions and the building drawings. The end user should take part in the discussion. Key inputs are the product types and the throughput (pieces per hour).
- **Brownfield (existing factory):** the concept is built around the current layout and running production.

## 4. Implementation
- **Handover:** after signing, the selected suppliers are contacted and a WeChat group is set up to manage the schedule. The implementation team takes over from the solution team.
- **AIMS on site:** AIMS sends **2–6 people**, typically a project manager and an implementation or software engineer.
- **Total team:** they lead a larger team of **20–40 people**, made up of supplier teams, a local team and an EPC company (the general engineering and installation contractor).
- **Equipment installed:** electrical systems, belts, conveyors and AGVs, step by step. Existing equipment is sometimes re-installed.
- **Main jobs:** **project management and supplier management** are the core of AIMS's role. Many suppliers must follow one precise time plan.
- **Surprises:** every project has them. In one project a transformer core fell during installation.
- **Duration:** installation normally takes **2–6 months**.

## 5. Acceptance, training and payment
- **Acceptance:** the customer comes to inspect. Normally they do not accept at the first inspection, and corrections take about another month. After corrections, the customer signs the acceptance, and payment follows.
- **Training:** given either before or after the factory acceptance test (FAT).
- **Payment:** in **USD**.
- **Where the value is:** AIMS says most of the value is created during **implementation and after-sales**, not in the concept.

## 6. International projects
For projects abroad, AIMS uses a **third-party EPC team** (one example you noted as "Orient") for mechanical and electrical work. **This is a possible role for Ulusoy in Türkiye**: local EPC, installation coordination and service.

## 7. References and capabilities relevant to us
- **Dalipal (steel):** a fully autonomous storage system for long pipes and large steel sheets.
- **Transformers:** AIMS can automate plants for both **oil-type transformers** (typically installed outdoors) and **dry-type transformers** (installed indoors).
- **Steel coils and sheets:** automated storage of steel sheet coils, and handling around **slitting machines** that cut coils into strips for transformer cores.
