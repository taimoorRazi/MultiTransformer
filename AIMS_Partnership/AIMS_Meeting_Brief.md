## 1. Who AIMS is (from the emails and their profile)

- **AIMS Technology Co., Ltd. (科大智能物联技术股份有限公司)**, Hefei and Shanghai. A smart-factory integrator: AS/RS, RGVs, AGVs, robotic workstations, vision inspection, plus its own software (AIMS-MOS, WMS, WCS, LES, uRMS, MES, APS).
- **Shareholders:** CSG Smart / Keda Smart Robot 29.08%; Chairman & GM Zhimin Zhong 19.70% plus partnerships he controls (about 38% more). iFlytek and others are financial investors only.
- **Sites:**
  - Hefei: metallurgy, packaging, new energy, automotive.
  - Shanghai: transformers, new energy; builds RGVs and lifts.
  - Zhangjiagang: process optimisation (software).
  - Jiangmen: WMS and MES.
- **Team:** about 390. Software 150, mechanical 60, electrical 55, project management 55, sales and solutions 40, admin 30.
- **Revenue (private, from Xindi):** US$75m (2023), US$120m (2024), US$120m (2025). 15–20% from transformer and power-equipment projects. They claim to have delivered more than 50% of China's projects in that sector.
- **Own products:** RGVs, part of the vision systems, PLC, press-fit stations, auto-polish stations, and all the software. Stacker cranes, racking and conveyors come from third parties.
- **Software licensing:** sold per module, with no limit on users. Connections go through APIs.
- **Export:** Thailand (Eaglerise, about 12 months from contract to final acceptance), Taiwan, Vietnam. CE is done by a third-party provider. Spare parts are included in the contract and kept on site. Remote support, with engineers sent when needed.
- **In Türkiye:** they once sold through a trader and don't know the end user. **They have no exclusive agreement in Türkiye and no agreement in the Middle East or Europe.**

## 4. What NOT to do

- **Don't name leads in the plenary.** Mr. Cem decides whether to name one; otherwise use a smaller session.
- **Don't quote AIMS's revenue or team numbers** back to them.
- **Don't ask for exclusivity or set a commission.** Agree simple rules for the first project; the full agreement comes after it.
- **Don't raise the TBEA Tianjin visit.** AIMS asked us to arrange that with TBEA directly (they value TBEA's trust).
- **Don't overstate Ulusoy Robotics' delivered projects.**
- **Don't cite BYD Manisa, the Ford–LG–Koç cell plant or Chery Samsun.** They are on hold or uncertain.

1. Can uRMS/WCS dispatch third-party heavy AGVs (Lonyu) through API? Has AIMS done this with another brand?
2. Are Turkish-language screens possible in WMS/MES, and at what cost and timing?
3. Which stacker-crane and racking suppliers would AIMS use for a European/Turkish project (CE-ready)?
4. Typical budget range for a transformer-plant intralogistics package (storage, RGVs, WMS/MES), so we can qualify leads.
5. Payment terms and warranty AIMS normally offers on export projects.
6. Who leads the first survey on the AIMS side, and how quickly can they travel?
7. How do they want to handle quotations: AIMS as contractor with Ulusoy as local partner, or Ulusoy as contracting party?
8. Would they support a reference visit for a Turkish customer to a Chinese transformer plant (not necessarily TBEA)?
