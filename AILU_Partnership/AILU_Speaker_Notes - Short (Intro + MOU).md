# AILU Meeting, Shanghai — Speaker Script (Short version: introduction + MOU)

**Deck:** AILU × Ulusoy — Introduction & MOU (25 slides, including 2 section dividers; no "Why we are here" slide; hospitality slide added as slide 11)
**Audience:** AILU's sales manager and international trade team
**Target length:** 30–40 minutes plus discussion. Keep the Ulusoy history slides (3–15) to about 8–9 minutes.
**Purpose:** Introduce Ulusoy and its strength, explain our plan in outline, and ask for agreement to work together under an MOU. No research figures are presented; the market study is the first deliverable after the MOU.

**If time is cut to 20 minutes, use this short path:** 1, 3, 6, 8, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25.

**Rules for the whole meeting**
1. **Do not present research numbers.** If asked, give only general impressions (e.g. "PV frames and specialty sacks look promising") and say the proper study comes under the MOU. The full research and the detailed deck are kept as backup.
2. **Do not mention AILU's weaknesses** (2025 loss, idle capacity, rating downgrade). They are our leverage, not talking points.
3. **Do not commit to exclusivity, volumes, stock or commission today.** The ask is the MOU and contact persons.
4. **Offer the bilingual MOU draft within one week.**

## Slide 1 — Cover: AILU × Ulusoy — A Partnership for Türkiye and the Region

**Main message:** An introduction and a proposal to work together — not a sales pitch.

**Script:**
"Good morning, and thank you for receiving us. Yesterday we visited your plant and showroom, and we were impressed by the automation, the REVOPAC lines, the IKKO tubes and the new PV frames. Today we would like to introduce Ulusoy properly, explain how we could support AILU in Türkiye and the region, and propose a simple first step to work together. AILU has the products — sacks, packing lines, tubes and PV frames. Ulusoy has the market access, the network and the local supply chain in Türkiye."

**Emphasise:** Thanks for the plant visit; mention one specific thing you saw.
**Do not dwell:** 30 seconds.

**Transition:** "Here is how we have organised today."

**Likely AILU question:** "What exactly do you want from us?"
**Suggested response:** "Today, only one thing: agreement to work together under a simple MOU, so we can start the market work. Everything else follows from the results."

## Slide 3 — 1937–2026 (Section 01: Who is Ulusoy)

**Main message:** Ulusoy is an 89-year family group — a long-term partner, not a trader looking for one deal.

**Script:**
"Ulusoy was founded in 1937 with one bus. Today the group has almost nine decades of history, three generations of family leadership, more than sixteen sectors and more than fifty years of international business. For a supplier like AILU, this matters: industrial customers in Türkiye buy packaging for years. They need a local partner who will still be there next year, and in ten years."

**Do not dwell:** 40 seconds.

**Transition:** "The group started with a very practical problem."

**Likely AILU question:** "Which Ulusoy company would we work with?"
**Suggested response:** "We would agree that together. It would be a group company with import and distribution capability. The group stands behind it." (Confirm internally beforehand.)

## Slide 4 — Origins: a transport problem solved, a principle established

**Main message:** "Everything on Time" applies directly to packaging supply.

**Script:**
"In the 1920s our region on the Black Sea had no roads. Our founder solved this first with a raft, then with trade, and in 1937 with the first Ulusoy coach. His principle was Everything on Time. In packaging this is critical: a cement plant or a food factory runs continuous filling lines. If the sacks are late, the line stops. That is the discipline we bring to supply in Türkiye."

**Emphasise:** "If the sacks are late, the line stops."

**Transition:** "Three generations then built on this principle."

## Slide 7 — Operating DNA: moving people and freight on schedule

**Main message:** Ulusoy has run national-scale operations: fleets, terminals, people and schedules.

**Script:**
"At its peak, our coach business carried three million passengers a year, with 450 coaches and 2,000 people. In the 1970s we had the largest coach fleet in Türkiye. This built something that still matters today: relationships in every major Turkish city, and the discipline of running a complex operation every day."

**Do not dwell:** These are historical peak figures. If asked, be clear that the coach business is not the group's core today.

**Likely AILU question:** "Is Ulusoy still in passenger transport?"
**Suggested response:** "The coaching brand built the group's name, but the group has moved on to maritime, ports, energy and technology. What remains is the network and the operating discipline."

**Transition:** "From national transport we went international."

## Slide 8 — International logistics: Türkiye–Europe–Middle East since 1973

**Main message:** Cross-border trade, customs and warehousing are native to Ulusoy — the backbone of importing packaging.

**Script:**
"Since 1973 we have run international transport between Türkiye, Europe and the Middle East, with offices in Munich, Trieste and London. In 1994 we added containers and bonded warehousing. For AILU this matters directly: importing sacks and frames means customs, port handling, warehousing and on-time delivery to the customer's plant. This is something our group does in-house."

**Emphasise:** Customs, bonded warehousing, local delivery.

**Transition:** "We also took the business to sea."

## Slide 9 — Maritime and Ro-Ro: operating international infrastructure

**Main message:** Ulusoy has operated international maritime infrastructure, and was already working with Chinese industry in 2008.

**Script:**
"Ulusoy Shipping started in 1981. Our Ro-Ro lines connected Türkiye with Russia and Ukraine across the Black Sea. At the peak we had eight vessels with about 194,000 tonnes of bulk capacity. One detail matters for this room: our first dry-cargo vessels in 2008 were built in China. So our working relationship with Chinese industry did not start this year. It started almost twenty years ago."

**Emphasise:** The China-built vessels; this builds rapport.

**Transition:** "Our most important maritime asset is a corridor to Europe."

## Slide 10 — Çeşme–Trieste: port ownership and cross-border trade

**Main message:** Ulusoy has developed and operated long-term infrastructure across borders: a port concession and an Italian company.

"In 2000 we opened the Çeşme–Trieste corridor between Türkiye and Italy. In 2003 we acquired the operating rights for Çeşme Port through privatization. We then extended the piers and set up Trieste Gateway in Italy. This shows we know how to win a concession, invest in infrastructure and operate it for decades under European standards."

**Do not dwell:** Do not go into vessel details.

**Likely AILU question:** "Does Ulusoy still operate the port and corridor today?"
**Suggested response:** Confirm the current status accurately before the meeting. Do not over-claim. If the status has changed, say: "This is part of our operating history. What it gave us is the experience and the relationships."

**Transition:** "From infrastructure, the group also moved into hospitality."

**Do not dwell:** On current ownership of the hotels; if asked, say they are part of the group's operating history. (Confirm the current status beforehand.)

**Transition:** "Infrastructure and hospitality are one side. The other is our history of representing international brands."

**Likely AILU question:** "Does the group still operate these hotels?"
**Suggested response:** "They are part of our operating history. What matters for this partnership is the experience and the relationships they built." (Adjust to the actual current status.)

When an industrial buyer talks to us about supply security and on-time delivery, we understand their problem as owners."

## Slide 19 — Four steps from market study to repeat orders

**Main message:** Ulusoy leads the market work; AILU supports with products, data and samples.

**Script:**
"Our plan has four steps. First, in weeks one to eight, a market study: demand by sector and product, local and imported competitors, price levels and buyer expectations, and duties and import rules. Second, from week six, network outreach: we introduce AILU to buyers in our network, select target accounts for each product line, visit them together, and invite key buyers to visit AILU in Shanghai. Third, from month three, samples and trials on customers' lines, quality approval and feedback to AILU. Fourth, from month six, first orders, with Ulusoy handling import, customs, warehousing, local delivery and after-sales."

**Emphasise:** "We do the market work." AILU's effort is products, data and samples.
**Do not present:** Research numbers. Our study will come after the MOU.

**Transition:** "What exactly would we study?"

**Likely AILU question:** "Can you already tell us which products will sell?"
**Suggested response:** "We have first impressions. PV frames and specialty sacks look promising, for example. But we would rather give you a proper study than a quick opinion. That is the first deliverable under the MOU."

## Slide 22 — A Memorandum of Understanding as the first step

**Main message:** A simple, non-binding MOU lets both sides start now.

**Script:**
"We propose a Memorandum of Understanding. Its purpose: to cooperate in developing AILU's business in Türkiye and the region. The scope: the AILU product lines and territories we agree together. The roles: AILU provides products, data, samples and pricing; Ulusoy does the market study, finds customers, and handles import and local supply. A joint plan: the market study and an entry plan within about two months. Customer protection: customers introduced by Ulusoy are registered and protected. And simple terms: confidentiality, non-binding, one to two years, with a distribution agreement as the next step. We can send a bilingual English–Chinese draft within one week."

**Emphasise:** "Non-binding" and "within one week". Make it easy to say yes.
**Do not push:** Exclusivity, commission or volumes today.

**Transition:** "Here is how it could grow from there."

**Likely AILU question:** "Do you want exclusivity in Türkiye?"
**Suggested response:** "Not now. We ask that customers we introduce are registered and protected. Broader terms can follow in the distribution agreement, once results are visible."
