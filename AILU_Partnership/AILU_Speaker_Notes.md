# AILU Meeting, Shanghai — Speaker Script

**Deck:** AILU × Ulusoy (36 slides, including 3 short section dividers)
**Audience:** AILU's sales manager and international trade team
**Target length:** 45–55 minutes plus discussion. Keep the Ulusoy history slides (4–15) short — about 6–8 minutes in total.
**Language:** Speak slowly and simply. Pause after every number.

**If time is cut to 25 minutes, use this short path:** 1, 2, 4, 9, 12, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 26, 27, 28, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36.

**The context in one paragraph**
We visited AILU's plant and showroom: high-speed sack lines with flexo printing, REVOPAC filling-line models, IKKO cosmetic tubes and composite PV frames. Our market study (internal) concluded that standard cement sacks will not win in Türkiye, but PV composite frames (after Türkiye's June 2026 anti-dumping duty on Chinese aluminium frames) and specialty sacks (food-grade, heat-seal, PE-lined, for building chemicals, food and chemicals) can. This presentation positions Ulusoy as the partner that opens Türkiye for those products, through a focused market test.

**Rules for the whole meeting**
1. **Do not mention AILU's weaknesses:** the 2025 loss, idle capacity, the rating downgrade or the PV-film subsidiary's bankruptcy. We know them; they are our leverage, not talking points.
2. **Do not quote AILU's average selling price** or our landed-cost calculation. Ask for their price indications instead.
3. **Do not promise cement-sack volume.** Our credibility comes from being honest about it.
4. **Do not say composite frames are duty-free.** Say "likely outside the duty, to be confirmed by a binding customs ruling".
5. **Do not commit to exclusivity, stock investment or commission levels today.** Agree a market test first.
6. **Do not present the named Turkish companies as our customers.** They are examples of each segment.

## Slide 20 — Standard sacks: a mature, export-oriented local industry

**Main message:** Standard cement sacks are a local, price-driven business. We will not promise volume there.

**Script:**

## Slide 22 — PV frames: a new opening since June 2026

**Main message:** Türkiye's anti-dumping duty on Chinese aluminium frames creates an opening for composite frames.

**Script:**
"In June 2026 Türkiye imposed a definitive anti-dumping duty of 38 to 46 percent on Chinese aluminium PV frames, for five years. Before that, China supplied about three quarters of Türkiye's frame imports. Türkiye has 13.4 gigawatts of module capacity, mostly for export and increasingly to the US — led by Smart Solar, Kalyon, CW Enerji, Kıvanç and Schmid-Pekintaş. The frame is about 14 percent of module cost, the largest single item. Why does AILU's composite frame fit now? The duty names aluminium frames, so a glass-fibre polyurethane frame is likely outside it — we would confirm this with a binding customs ruling. It is lighter and corrosion-resistant. And module makers would need to re-certify modules, so certificates and test data decide."

**Emphasise:** "Likely outside it — to be confirmed." Do not promise it is duty-free.

**Transition:** "Two smaller openings: packing lines and cosmetic tubes."

**Likely AILU question:** "How much can Turkish module makers buy?"
**Suggested response:** "Each gigawatt needs roughly two million frames, depending on module size. Even a small share of 13 gigawatts is significant. The first step is trials with two or three makers." (Frames per GW is an approximation; confirm with AILU's own numbers.)

## Slide 24 — Trade rules to plan for — before the first quotation

**Main message:** Duties and trade defence must be checked product by product. Ulusoy will do this.

**Script:**
"Paper sacks carry zero customs duty, plastic sacks 6.5 percent and woven sacks 7.2 percent. But Türkiye also applies an additional duty on many products from countries without a trade agreement, which includes China; the exact rate must be confirmed for each product. There are anti-dumping duties on Chinese BOPP film and aluminium PV frames, import surveillance on sack kraft, and a safeguard investigation on paper and board. For composite frames we recommend a binding customs ruling before the first shipment. State solar tenders reward domestic components. Ulusoy will confirm every rate with a customs broker before we quote."

**Emphasise:** "Before we quote." This protects both sides.
