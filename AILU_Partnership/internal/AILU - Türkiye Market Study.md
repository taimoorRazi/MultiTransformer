## 2. AILU in brief: what they have, and what they need

**What they have**

- **Listed company:** Shenzhen 301062. 2025 revenue RMB 1.174bn; 9M 2026 revenue RMB 1.004bn (+13.9%), back to profit.
- **Exports:** RMB 287m in 2025 (24% of revenue), with a gross margin of about 27% versus 18.6% for the group.
- **Five product lines and their subsidiaries:**
  - industrial paper sacks (about 70–76% of revenue);
  - plastic and barrier films;
  - **REVOPAC** packing machines (wholly owned; H1 2026 +274%);
  - **IKKO** cosmetic and pharma tubes and jars (controlling stake);
  - **Aina** PV composite frames (51.5%; 1.8 GW capacity; VDE and TÜV SÜD certificates; volume shipping since Nov 2026).
- **Plant:** about 600m sacks/yr of capacity on 10 high-speed W&H-type lines (up to 400 sacks/min), with an AIMS smart warehouse, AGVs and MES.
- **Certificates:** ISO 9001, ISO 22000, HACCP, BRCGS Packaging. Co-developed paper barrier packaging with Mondelez.
- **Customers:** Nippon Paint, Davco, Oriental Yuhong, Skshu, Wacker, Cargill, FrieslandCampina, Olam, Nestlé. More than 700 customers.

**What they need (our leverage — don't say it in the room)**

- **Idle capacity:** about 288m sacks produced in 2025 against roughly 600m capacity, about 48% utilisation (L). Sack sales fell 11%.
- **Weaker credit:** a 2025 net loss of RMB 123m, and a rating cut from A+ to A in June 2026.
- **PV problems:** the backsheet-film subsidiary is filing for bankruptcy, and the frame subsidiary is still loss-making.
- **Export push:** management targets 70–100% export growth a year.
- **No Türkiye presence:** no Turkish sales, agent or office, but the website already has a Turkish-language section, updated January 2026.
- **New chair:** Chen Xueqi has been Chair and General Manager since May 2026, and may want visible new markets.

## 3. The Turkish sack market

### Demand

- **Cement:**
  - About 85–94 Mt produced in 2025, a record (the exact figure is unconfirmed).
  - Domestic sales were about 69 Mt in 2023.
  - Only about **16–19% is sold in bags** (8.7 Mt in 2021). Exports (15.5 Mt) go in bulk.
  - That gives roughly 220–290m 50 kg cement sacks a year (L).
- **Other sack users:**
  - dry-mix mortar and tile adhesives in 25 kg sacks (Kalekim, Weber, Baumit, Sika, Mapei);
  - flour: Türkiye is the world's no. 1 flour exporter, at 2.3 Mt;
  - feed: 30.7 Mt;
  - fertiliser: 6.5 Mt;
  - sugar: state buyers;
  - dairy, food ingredients and chemicals: no data found, but this is AILU's best niche.

### Supply

- **Captive cement groups:**
  - OYAK makes its own kraft and sacks through **OYKA**: 100 kt of paper, 220m sacks.
  - **Çimsa** makes its own sacks in Mersin.
- **Merchant converters:**
  - Segezha (Russian-owned, ex-Işıklar; İzmir and Elazığ; about 300m sacks), now financially stressed;
  - Mondi (Çorlu);
  - Yozgat Kraft, Türk Kraft, Ekerler and others.
  - Named capacity exceeds 900m sacks/yr (L), well above domestic demand.
- **PP woven sacks:** a large cluster in Gaziantep and Adana. Türkiye is a top-5 world exporter.
- **Sack kraft paper is mostly imported** (US, Russia, Nordics, Poland). Since January 2026 it has been under **import surveillance**.

### Trade

- **Exports:** Türkiye exported **US$170m / 73 kt of paper sacks** (HS 481940) in 2023, at about US$2.32/kg.
- **Imports:** only about **6 kt** in 2024, at about US$3.55/kg. The imports are higher-value specialty sacks.
- **Gap:** China's share of these imports was not found.

### Duties (confirm with a Turkish customs broker)

- **Paper sacks:** 0% customs duty, plus Türkiye's **additional duty on non-FTA countries (İGV)**. This is possibly about 25% for China, based on the neighbouring paper-box code; it is unconfirmed for sacks.
- **Plastic sacks:** 6.5%; **PP woven:** 7.2%, plus İGV of about 20%.
- **No anti-dumping duty found on sacks.** There is anti-dumping on Chinese BOPP film (12.85–62.94%), and a safeguard investigation on paper and board was extended in October 2026.
- **VAT:** 20% (recoverable by the importer).
- **EU suppliers (Mondi and others) pay none of these extra duties.**

## 4. Landed-cost check: standard 50 kg cement sack (L, indicative)

| Item | Assumption | US$ per sack |
|---|---|---|
| AILU FOB price | AILU's average selling price is about RMB 2.8–3.1 (US$0.38–0.42) | 0.38–0.42 |
| Sea freight Shanghai → Mersin | US$4,400 per 40 ft (Nov 2026) ÷ about 100–120k sacks per container | ~0.04 |
| Insurance, port, customs clearance, inland | about US$700–1,000 per container | ~0.01 |
| İGV (if 25% applies) | 25% of the CIF value | 0 – 0.11 |
| **Landed cost** | | **≈ 0.43 – 0.58** |
| **Turkish benchmark** | Turkish export unit value | **≈ 0.32 – 0.40** |

**Conclusion:** at AILU's normal prices a standard cement sack lands **10–45% above** the Turkish benchmark.

**Break-even FOB price:**

- about **US$0.30** if there is no İGV;
- about **US$0.24** if 25% İGV applies.

Before any importer margin. AILU's idle capacity might justify aggressive marginal pricing, but a surge of cheap Chinese sacks would invite an anti-dumping complaint from OYKA or other local converters. **Don't build the business on cement sacks.**

**Specialty sacks are different:**

- Türkiye's imports are priced about 50% higher per kg than its exports.
- Food-grade, heat-seal and PE-lined sacks carry a premium.
- Buyers choose on qualification (food contact, BRCGS) more than on price.

## 5. PV composite frames: the lead product

- **The anti-dumping duty:** Türkiye imposed a definitive duty on Chinese **aluminium** PV frames on 24 Jun 2026 (Communiqué 2026/23). It is **38.26–45.99% of CIF**, for 5 years, under tariff line 8541.90.00.00.11. Provisional duties since Dec 2025 had been 14.79–31.40%. China supplied about 76% of frame imports.
- **Why AILU's frame may escape it:** it is glass-fibre polyurethane, not aluminium, so it is probably outside the measure. **Get a binding customs classification ruling (BTI)** before the first shipment.
- **Buyers:** about 13.2–13.4 GW/yr of module capacity, more than the EU-27. The largest makers:
  - Smart Solar 2.4 GW;
  - Kalyon PV 2.1 GW;
  - CW Enerji 1.8 GW;
  - Kıvanç 1.2 GW;
  - Schmid-Pekintaş 1.2 GW.
  - Most output is exported, increasingly to the US.
- **Value:** aluminium frames are about **14% of module cost**, the largest single item.
- **Risks:**
  - Composite frames are not yet fully proven or accepted by banks; modules must be re-certified with a new frame.
  - YEKA tenders and the YEKDEM bonus favour domestic parts.
  - Turkish aluminium extruders (some owned by module makers, e.g. CW Enerji) may push to extend the duty to composite frames.
- **Medium-term best route:** **local assembly or licensing in Türkiye** with a Turkish partner. That creates domestic content and avoids trade defence. Ulusoy could play a real role here.

## 6. Packing lines (REVOPAC) and cosmetic tubes (IKKO)

- **REVOPAC:**
  - Valve-bag fillers, robotic bag placers, palletisers, stretch-hood and big-bag lines.
  - In Türkiye they compete with European suppliers (Haver & Boecker, BEUMER, Concetti), whose pricing is high.
  - The pitch is "bag + filling line from one supplier".
  - Targets: dry-mix mortar plants (Kalekim is expanding), fertiliser plants, food plants.
  - Capital equipment carries different duty treatment; confirm it.
- **IKKO:**
  - Türkiye's cosmetics exports were US$2.33bn in 2025 (+11.8%), and the domestic market is about US$3.3bn.
  - Local converters are strong (ALPLA, Rosti, Perfektüp and others).
  - Openings: premium laminated tubes and sustainable tubes for export-oriented brands (Eczacıbaşı, Evyap, Farmasi, Flormar, Golden Rose and others).
  - Low priority for now. Only a 4% margin for AILU on this line.
