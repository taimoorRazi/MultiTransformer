# Türkiye industrial sack market: can Shanghai Ailu Package (AILU) export there?

*Prepared 2026-11-22 as a briefing for the Turkish team meeting AILU on 2026-11-23.*

**How to read this**
- **Confidence:** **H** = an official or primary source, or several sources agree. **M** = one credible secondary source, or a company's own claim. **L** = a weak, old or conflicting source, or my own estimate.

## 0. Executive summary (one paragraph)

Türkiye is a large market for sacks. Domestic cement sales are about 69–75 Mt a year, and roughly 16–20% of that is sold in bags. The country also has very large flour, feed, fertiliser and dry-mix mortar industries. But Türkiye is a **net exporter** of both paper sacks and plastic/PP woven sacks, with local overcapacity:
- Exports of HS 481940 alone were **US$170M / 73 kt in 2023**.
- Imports of paper sacks are only about **6 kt a year**.

The largest cement group (**OYAK**) has its own integrated sack-kraft mill and sack plant (**OYKA**: 100 kt of paper, 220M sacks a year). **Çimsa** makes its own sacks. Other cement producers buy from domestic converters: Segezha (ex-Işıklar), Mondi, Yozgat Kraft, Ekerler, Türk Kraft and others. These buyers say 85–97% of their supplier spend is local.

For Chinese finished sacks, the barriers are:
- an import duty structure that probably adds an extra duty on non-FTA origin (about 25% indicated on HS 4819, but not verified for 4819.40);
- sea freight of roughly US$4,000–4,500 per 40 ft container;
- 6–8 weeks of transit and planning, against just-in-time local supply;
- Türkiye's active trade-defence posture: anti-dumping duties on Chinese BOPP film, import surveillance on sack kraft since 30 Jan 2026, and an ongoing safeguard investigation on paper and board.

**Realistic openings for AILU:**
1. Niche or specialty sacks: heat-seal, PE-lined or barrier sacks for food ingredients, milk powder and chemicals, SOS/square-bottom formats, and AD*STAR-type block-bottom PP sacks.
2. Supplying sack kraft or semi-finished material to Turkish converters, if the surveillance regime allows it.
3. Using Turkish partners as a hub for third markets such as Iraq, Syria and Africa.

Commodity 50 kg cement valve sacks sold against OYKA, Segezha and Mondi are a hard sell.

- **Trend:** The bagged share is falling as ready-mix concrete and bulk sales grow. Ready-mix takes about 44–50% of domestic cement, and the dealer channel about 36%.
- **Estimate (L):** At a 16–18% bagged share of 69–75 Mt domestic sales, about **11–13 Mt of cement is sold in bags each year**. At 50 kg per bag that is roughly **220–270 million cement sacks a year**, before waste. TOBB's capacity norm assumes 22 paper bags per tonne of cement, including 10% waste ([TOBB](https://tobb.org.tr/SanayiMudurlugu/Documents/KapasiteKriterleri/grup3692.pdf)), which pushes the figure toward 240–290M.
- **Exports:** Not quantified. The US flows (7.5 Mt) are bulk. Some exports to Africa, Syria and Iraq may be bagged, but no data was found.

### 1.3 Bag sizes and paper vs PP

- **50 kg is the standard Turkish cement bag.** An old Ministry of Environment & Urbanisation specification sets net weight at 50 kg ±2% and requires cement in closed, undamaged **paper bags** carrying the information TS EN 197-1 requires ([ÇŞB spec](https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/yfk/icerikler/c-mento-20230202145324.pdf)). (M; the document is old.)
- **25 kg** is common for dry-mix mortars, tile adhesives and building chemicals. Local converters list 25/35/40/50 kg valve sacks and 2–5 ply builds ([Türk Kraft](https://turkkraft.com.tr/tr/landing/kraft-torba); [ForaysKraft](https://www.forayskraft.com/valfli-kraft-torba-ureticisi/)). (M)
- **No Turkish rule found that forces 50 kg cement bags down to 25 kg.** Mexico is doing this under NOM-036 ([Global Cement](https://www.globalcement.com/news/item/17193-cement-firms-shift-to-lighter-bags-for-worker-health)). A future Turkish move toward 25 kg would raise sack counts. (L, as an opportunity to watch.)
- **Paper vs PP woven:** No published split was found. The evidence points to **multi-wall paper valve sacks dominating domestic cement**:
  - the standard specification refers to paper bags;
  - all the large converters are kraft-sack makers;
  - OYAK and Çimsa run their own paper-sack plants.
- **PP woven cement and lime bags** are offered by Abdioğulları and others ([Ekonomist](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/makale/yarim-asirdir-cuval-oruyor-469)). They are probably used mainly for export and humid or long-haul markets. (M/L)
- **Filling performance:** A CEPI Eurokraft lab test found paper sacks filled about **21% faster** than woven PP and lost less cement ([Eurosac/ICR 2025](https://www.eurosac.org/fileadmin/pdf/eurosac_in_the_media/2025-11_ICR-REPRINT_CEPI-Eurokraft-Packaging.pdf)). This is industry-funded, so treat it as M.

## 4. Costs, prices, duties

### 4.1 Sack prices

- **No published Turkish price per cement sack was found.**
- **Implied Turkish export unit value (H data, L estimate):** About US$2.32/kg for HS 481940 (2023). A 50 kg 2-ply sack of about 80 g/m² paper weighs roughly 0.14–0.17 kg (my assumption, not sourced), so this implies **about US$0.32–0.40 per sack FOB, or about US$320–400 per 1,000**.
- **Older Turkish cost hint (L):** TÜRKÇİMENTO once put "sack paper, raw material and labour" at TL 106 per tonne of cement, with sack paper bought in euros. The date is unclear and the figure is now obsolete ([TOBB](https://tobb.org.tr/SanayiMudurlugu/Documents/KapasiteKriterleri/grup3692.pdf) and press).

### 4.5 Rough landed-cost comparison (estimate, L)

- **Chinese 2-ply 50 kg kraft valve sack:** About $0.18 FOB, plus about $0.035 freight and insurance, then 0% duty plus a possible 25% İGV. That gives about **$0.27 per sack** before clearance, inland logistics, inventory and claims cost.
- **Turkish comparison:** Turkish export unit value is about $0.32–0.40.
- **What this means:** On paper, Chinese sacks *could* undercut. The margin narrows once you add:
  - quality risk (paper porosity and strength);
  - 6–10 weeks of pipeline stock;
  - FX exposure;
  - the risk of a Turkish anti-dumping case. Domestic producers are vocal and have succeeded on kraft and film.

## 5. Quality, standards, buyer expectations

- **Cement:** TS EN 197-1 governs the cement itself and the information printed on bags (type and class, producer, conformity marking). Bags must be closed and undamaged, and the old spec gives net weight as 50 kg ±2% ([ÇŞB](https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/yfk/icerikler/c-mento-20230202145324.pdf)). (M)
- **Sack standards:**
  - **ISO 6590-1** (paper sack vocabulary; 2nd edition 2025) ([ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/87607.html)).
  - **ISO 8367-1 / EN ISO 8367-1** (dimensional tolerances: ply grammage, length, width, gusset, valve; new edition July 2026) ([en-standard.eu](https://www.en-standard.eu/iso-8367-1-2026-packaging-dimensional-tolerances-for-general-purpose-sacks-part-1-paper-sacks/)).
  - Turkish TS EN adoption status not verified. (M)
- **Filling speed and breakage (buyer KPIs):**
  - A breathable 25 kg paper cement sack fills in about 4–4.5 s on rotary packers ([Eurosac/ICR](https://www.eurosac.org/fileadmin/pdf/press_articles/ICR-REPRINT_2023-04_Eurosac_Bagging.pdf)).
  - Sack venting (air permeability, Gurley; "high-porosity" sack kraft is under 7 Gurley s) is often the line-speed limit ([Billerud Sack Handbook](https://www.billerud.com/globalassets/billerud/packaging-materials/sack-paper/sack-handbook-251218.pdf)).
  - LCA assumptions for life-cycle breakage: 2-ply paper 2.8%, 3-ply paper 2.3%, woven PP 3.8–4.4%, AD*STAR 1% ([PackagingEurope](https://www.packagingeurope.com/packaging-cement-the-sustainable-way.md)). (M; vendor-linked sources)
- **Typical complaints about imported sacks:** No Turkish-specific evidence was found (unverified). From general industry practice (L), the usual issues are:
  - inconsistent porosity, which slows filling and causes bursts or dust;
  - valve dimension drift on the packer spout;
  - curl or moisture after a long sea voyage;
  - print colour mismatch against brand standards.
- **Lead time (M, inferred):** Local converters deliver in days and hold buffer stock at the plant. Cement demand is seasonal (Q1 2026 domestic sales fell 12% on rain). A China supply chain needs about 6–8 weeks of transit plus production, so the buyer carries the inventory and forecast risk.
- **Buyer posture:** Nuh Çimento reports **85.79% of supplier spend is domestic** (2024 TSRS report) and lists "Big Bag/Torba" purchasing in its supply chain ([Nuh 2024 TSRS](https://www.kgk.gov.tr/Portalv2Uploads/files/Duyurular/v2/Surdurulebilirlik/Raporlar/NuhCimentoGrubu2024TSRSUyumluSurdurulebilirlikRaporu.pdf)). Akçansa reports about **97% local suppliers** (2018–19) ([Akçansa SR](https://www.akcansa.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/SR2018-2019.pdf)). (H)

## Can AILU export sacks to Türkiye? Evidence for and against

### For
1. **Big and growing end markets:**
   - Cement output was a record 85–94 Mt in 2025, with about 11–13 Mt sold bagged.
   - Feed 30.7 Mt, flour about 15 Mt (no. 1 world exporter), fertiliser 6.5 Mt consumption.
   - Türkiye is Europe's no. 3 paper-sack market.
   - A large and expanding dry-mix mortar sector (e.g. Kalekim's new plants).
2. **Paper sacks carry 0% customs duty** under the CET.
3. **Turkish converters depend on imported sack kraft**, and now face import surveillance on it plus euro/rouble cost exposure. Turkish sack prices (implied ~US$0.32–0.40 per 50 kg sack at export unit value) leave theoretical room for a Chinese offer at about US$0.27 landed before İGV clearance costs. (L estimate)
4. **Segezha (Russia), a major local converter, is financially stressed**, which could create supply-security worries for its cement customers.
5. **Niche fit:** heat-seal, PE-lined, square-bottom and SOS sacks for food ingredients, milk powder and chemicals are less commoditised and less served by cement-focused converters. A regulatory move toward 25 kg cement bags (as in Mexico) would raise sack counts.
6. **Türkiye as a hub:** Turkish partners export sacks and packed goods (flour to Syria, cement to Africa and Iraq). AILU could supply partners or co-pack for third markets.

### Against
1. **Türkiye is a net exporter with overcapacity:**
   - HS 481940 exports US$170M / 73 kt in 2023, against paper-sack imports of only about 6 kt.
   - About 223 kt of plastic sack exports.
   - A world top-5 PP woven exporter.
2. **The two biggest cement groups are captive:** OYAK through OYKA (220M sacks and Türkiye's only integrated kraft mill), and Çimsa through its own Mersin plant. Others report 85–97% local supplier spend.
3. **Duty asymmetry:**
   - Chinese goods likely face İGV (about 25% shown on 4819.10; reportedly ~20% on 6305 woven sacks; 5–15% on plastics), while EU and FTA suppliers (Mondi, Billerud-linked converters) pay 0%.
   - Add 6.5–7.2% customs duty on plastic and woven sacks, and 20% VAT.
   - Türkiye already imposes AD duties on Chinese BOPP film (12.85–62.94%) and on 557 Chinese tariff lines.
4. **Active trade defence on paper:**
   - A safeguard on fluting and testliner since 2024.
   - Surveillance on sack kraft since Jan 2026.
   - An open safeguard investigation on paper and board, extended Oct 2026.
   - Domestic producers (OYKA) have a track record of filing. A surge of Chinese sacks would likely trigger an AD case.
5. **Logistics and lead time:** Freight is about US$4,400 per 40 ft (Nov 2026) and volatile because of Middle East risk. The 6–10 week pipeline competes against local just-in-time delivery for seasonal cement demand.
6. **Quality qualification:** Packers run at high speed and are sensitive to sack porosity, valve fit and breakage, so trials are long. Public buyers (Türkşeker, TMO) use domestic-preference tenders.

**Bottom line (M):** Mass-market cement valve sacks are **unlikely to win** except as occasional spot or overflow supply. A **targeted entry is plausible** with:
- specialty multi-wall paper sacks: heat-seal, PE-lined, food-grade, SOS, for dairy, food-ingredient, chemical and dry-mix mortar multinationals;
- possibly AD*STAR-type block-bottom PP sacks.

This should be tested through a Turkish distributor or partner. First confirm the exact 12-digit GTİP duties (İGV and AD) with a Turkish customs broker, and check that no AD investigation is pending.
