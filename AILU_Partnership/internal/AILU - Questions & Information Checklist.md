# AILU Meeting — Questions & Information Checklist

**Purpose:** collect everything we need to find the right customers in Türkiye for AILU.
**Tip:** ask for documents by email after the meeting ("please send to…"). In the room, focus on the questions marked ★.

---

## A. Documents to request (by email after the meeting)

1. ★ **Product catalogues** for every line, in English: paper sacks, plastic packaging, REVOPAC machines, IKKO tubes, PV frames.
2. ★ **Technical data sheets:**
   - **Sacks:** paper grades, number of plies, liner options, valve types, sizes, load range.
   - **Machines:** speed, bag types and sizes, power, footprint.
   - **Frames:** dimensions, weight, mechanical tests.
3. ★ **Certificates:** ISO 9001, ISO 22000, HACCP, BRCGS, and any other certificates or audit reports.
4. **Food-contact compliance** (EU 10/2011 or equivalent) and migration test reports for food sacks and films.
5. **PV frame certification pack:** VDE and TÜV SÜD certificates, and the IEC 61215/61730 module reports where the frame was used.
6. **REVOPAC CE declarations** and export installation list.
7. ★ **Reference list:** customers by industry and country (at least the names they allow us to use).
8. **Company presentation and videos** (plant, products, REVOPAC lines) for showing to Turkish buyers.
9. **Sample kit:** physical samples of the main sack types and tubes, plus one frame section if possible.
10. **Price lists or indicative export prices** in USD (FOB Shanghai), by product and quantity.

## B. General and strategy

1. ★ Which products does AILU most want to sell abroad, and which are its strongest?
2. ★ Which countries are their export priorities today, and where do they sell best?
3. ★ Have they received **enquiries from Türkiye** or the Middle East? From whom (sector, size)?
4. Why does their website have a **Turkish-language section**? Is Türkiye already on their plan?
5. Do they work with **distributors or agents** in other countries? How are those arrangements structured?
6. Who will be our **contact person**? Who decides on export partnerships?

## C. Paper sacks

1. ★ Which **sack types** do they export most (valve, square-bottom, heat-seal, sewn, pinch)?
2. ★ Which **industries** do they serve best abroad (cement, dry mix, food, dairy, chemicals, feed)?
3. ★ **Minimum order quantity** per size and per print design?
4. ★ **Lead time** from order to shipment, and how much capacity is available for export?
5. Can they make **small trial batches** for customer tests?
6. **Printing:** how many colours, print quality, and the cost of new printing plates?
7. **Paper:** which kraft paper they use (own or imported), and whether they offer extensible or high-porosity grades for fast filling.
8. How many sacks fit in a **40 ft container** for typical sizes, and how are they packed?
9. Typical **breakage rate** and filling speed achieved at customers?
10. Do they provide **technical support** for sack trials on the customer's filling machine?

## D. Plastic packaging

1. Which plastic products are for export: FFS heavy-duty film, barrier films, laminates, other?
2. Main end uses and customers abroad?
3. MOQ, lead time, and printing options?

## E. REVOPAC packing lines

1. ★ Product range and **typical project price ranges** (filler, bag placer, palletiser, complete line)?
2. ★ **Export installations:** which countries and customers? Any in Europe or the Middle East?
3. **CE marking:** do the machines have it, and can they supply full documentation?
4. **Installation and service abroad:** who installs, typical duration, remote support, spare parts?
5. Can they offer **"sack + filling line"** as one package, tested together?
6. Delivery time for a typical line?

## F. IKKO cosmetic tubes

1. Product range: tube types, diameters, laminate structures, PCR and sugar-cane options, jars?
2. ★ **MOQ per design** and decoration options (offset, silk screen, hot stamping)?
3. Main customers and export markets?
4. GMP and cosmetic-packaging certifications?

## G. PV composite frames

1. ★ **Price per frame (or per watt)** compared with aluminium frames?
2. ★ Which **module makers** already use their frames, and in which countries?
3. ★ **Certificates:** IEC 61215/61730 on which module designs; fire class; salt-mist, UV and creep tests.
4. Available **capacity for export** (of the 1.8 GW), and lead time?
5. Which **HS code** do they declare for export?
6. **Warranty** offered on the frame, and long-term test data?
7. Would they consider **local assembly or licensing** in Türkiye in future?

## H. Commercial terms

1. ★ **Payment terms** for export (deposit, letter of credit, open account)?
2. ★ **Incoterms:** FOB Shanghai, CIF Mersin or İstanbul?
3. Price validity, and how they adjust for paper and resin price changes?
4. Do they offer **special prices for market entry**, samples free of charge, or support for trials?
5. Who pays for samples and their shipping?

## I. Quality and claims

1. How do they handle **quality complaints** and claims abroad?
2. What **inspection reports** come with each shipment?
3. Are they open to **customer audits** of the plant?

## J. Partnership

1. ★ Are they open to an **MOU** with Ulusoy for Türkiye and the region?
2. Would they **protect customers** that Ulusoy introduces (registration)?
3. What **support** can they give: marketing material in English, joint visits to Türkiye, trade fairs?
4. Would they host **Turkish buyers** for plant visits in Shanghai?
5. **Next meeting:** when and where, and who should join from each side?

### The 10 most important to cover in the room (★)
1. Products AILU most wants to export.
2. Any Turkish or Middle East enquiries so far.
3. Sack types and industries they serve best abroad.
4. MOQ per design and lead time.
5. Indicative export prices (or agree when they will send them).
6. PV frame price vs aluminium, reference module makers, certificates.
7. REVOPAC export references and CE status.
8. Payment terms and Incoterms.
9. Openness to an MOU and customer protection.
10. Contact person, and the documents they will send.
