## 1. Business segments, revenue split, margins, exports

### 1.1 Segments (company's own definition)
The company describes five product lines: **industrial paper packaging; consumer-goods paper packaging; plastic packaging; intelligent packaging systems; PV new-material (module frames)**. It positions itself as an "integrated flexible/soft-packaging solution provider" for industrial and consumer packaging. (H) Sources: [2025 annual report summary, cnstock](https://paper.cnstock.com/html/2026-04/29/content_2209001.htm); [p5w H1 2026](https://www.p5w.net/kuaixun/202608/t20260817_6494225.htm)

Operating subsidiaries relevant to the meeting:
- **Shanghai Revopac Packaging Technology (上海锐派包装 / "REVOPAC")**: wholly owned. Filling, bagging and palletising machines. (H, see §2.4)
- **Shanghai IKKO New Material Technology (上海艾鲲新材料科技 / "IKKO", 艾鲲)**: controlling stake. Cosmetic, pharma and food tubes, injection-moulded jars and caps. (H, see §5)
- **Nantong Aina New Energy Technology (南通艾纳新能源)**: controlling stake. Polyurethane composite PV frames. (H, see §4)
- **Nantong Ailu New Energy Technology (南通艾录新能源)**: 73% stake. PV backsheet film; bankruptcy filing approved Oct 2026. (H)

## 2. Plants, capacity, utilisation, automation

### 2.1 Sites
- Three intelligent manufacturing bases, all in **Jinshan District, Shanghai**, organised in three production segments: industrial paper packaging, plastic packaging and intelligent packaging systems. Equipment includes cast-film, blown-film, sheet extrusion, printing, bag-making, tube-making and laminating lines. (H; 2025 annual-report summary via search)
- Registered HQ: 88 Yangle Road (阳乐路88号), Shanyang Town, Jinshan (H). IKKO (cosmetics tubes): 88 Yangda Road (阳达路88号), Shanyang Town, Jinshan (H).
- PV frames: Nantong Aina, Nantong, Jiangsu (H).
- **No overseas manufacturing found.** (M)

### 2.2 Paper-sack capacity and utilisation
- The convertible-bond (RMB 500m, listed 20 Nov 2023) expansion project adds **600m industrial paper sacks/yr at full output** (H; 2024 results briefing). Management statement of Nov 2026: "annual production of 600 million industrial paper packaging bags… existing 10 industrial paper tuber lines can support annual revenue of RMB 2.3–2.5bn" (H). This implies roughly **2× headroom over 2025 paper-sack revenue**. [Bigdata.com: Orient Securities plant visit, 16 Nov 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/187CF347F2D6D8D6EE7F6EA73A31E62F)
- New intelligent packaging production centre: **10 fully automatic bag-making lines, each worth ~RMB 100m, top speed 400 sacks/min, combined capacity "exceeding 1bn sacks/yr", with only 6–7 workers per shift per line** (H; same source). W&H (Windmöller & Hölscher, Germany) bag-making equipment has been used since 2006, with a W&H spare-parts plant in Taicang nearby (H).
- 2025 production was 288.15m sacks, so utilisation was roughly **48% of ~600m**, or under 30% of the theoretical 1bn line capacity (L; derived). Lianhe Ratings (June 2026) noted that **paper-sack utilisation declined and composite-plastic utilisation is low**, with "significant pressure to digest capacity", and downgraded the issuer and bond from A+ to **A** (outlook stable) (H). [Bigdata.com / Cailian](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/A12E0004221DA46E4FC67F428E289197)
- Chairman's stated priority (Nov 2026): "fully release existing capacity and improve utilisation". (H) **This is the key commercial lever for a Turkish distributor discussion: AILU needs volume.**

### 2.3 Printing and raw materials
- Printing: "new-generation **German** satellite (central-impression) flexo, 8 colours, 500 m/min, 650,000 m²/day" (H; plant-visit article). The visit video showed **EKOFA**-branded flexo presses. EKOFA is the press brand of **Qingzhou Multi-flex Machinery Co., Ltd. (China)**, which makes 1–12 colour presses, 200–1600 mm wide (M; [allinprint exhibitor listing](https://www.allinprint.com/en/exhibitorInfo.html?id=8926)). So the plant likely runs both German CI presses and Chinese EKOFA presses (L).
- Roll-paper automated high-bay warehouse: >2,400 m², 30 m high, ~9,500 rolls / 8,000 t of sack kraft (H).
- Kraft paper is price-locked annually with paper mills in Nov–Dec by specification. The company is raising the share of domestic paper and has completed domestic substitution for some high-spec sack kraft (previously imported) (H).

### 2.4 Automation and smart-warehouse supplier: **AIMS confirmed**
- **AIMS Technology (aimstek.cn; the user's "AIMS / 科大智能" lead) lists the project as a case study: "Assist Shanghai Ailu in creating intelligent packaging throughout the entire process."** Scope: an integrated warehousing-and-production digital system covering raw-material warehousing, production distribution and finished-goods outbound; intelligent warehouse linked to **AGV scheduling** for unmanned cross-workshop and cross-floor delivery; **MES** for scheduling, material call-off and process control; digital-twin and full-chain data collection for traceability. (H that AIMS supplied it) [AIMS case 576](https://www.aimstek.cn/en/show_case/576); [AIMS printing & packaging cases](https://www.aimstek.cn/en/CaseStudies/CS_PrintingPackaging)
- Caveat: I did not verify whether aimstek.cn is the same legal entity as the A-share company 科大智能 (CSG Smart Science & Technology). Treat "AIMS = 科大智能" as **M**.
- The showroom has a "digital dome" showing informatisation, data visualisation, intelligent manufacturing and digital twin (H).

## 3. Product range and technical features

### 3.1 Industrial multi-wall paper sacks (core business)
- Types: **pasted valve sacks; block (square) bottom; sewn open-mouth; pinch-bottom open-mouth; satchel-bottom open-mouth; heat-seal sacks**. Eco line: **AiTop** series (26.84% lower carbon per unit, company claim). (H) [ailupack pasted valve bags](https://www.ailupack.com/pasted-valve-bags/); [plant-visit article](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/187CF347F2D6D8D6EE7F6EA73A31E62F)
- **Pasted valve sack specs (company site):** width 180–740 mm; length 250–1310 mm; bottom 70–250 mm; **1–6 plies**; up to **8-colour** print; load **10–110 lb (~5–50 kg)**; bleached or unbleached base paper; options of polycoated layers, **HDPE film interlayer or PE inliner**, or **PE-free** constructions; perforated paper for fast de-aeration; tear strip; security label. (H)
- **Valve types:** paper insert, double trap, reinforced poly-lock, reduced valve, tuck-in sleeve, sonic-seal sleeve. **Closures:** ultrasonic sealing, heat sealing, self-sealing (plus the standard pressure-closing valve). Ultrasonically sealed extended valves with a "unique venting system" for dust-free, sift-proof filling of fine powders (e.g. nano carbon black, silica). (H) [ailupack homepage](https://www.ailupack.com/); [valve-bag article Apr 2025](https://www.ailupack.com/how-is-ailu-revolutionizing-the-powder-packaging-industry-with-valve-bags/)
- Open-mouth sacks: "multiple moisture-barrier options; leak-proof design". (H) [CHINAPLAS 2026 page](https://www.ailupack.com/ailu-at-chinaplas-2026-smart-packaging-solutions/)
- **End uses:** cement, putty and dry-mix mortar (heavy-duty, waterproof); chemicals (titanium dioxide, carbon black, silica, resins: "corrosion/acid-resistant, superior sealing"); food and food additives, dairy and milk powder, flour, starch ("FDA-compliant"); pharma intermediates (ultra-barrier); feed/agri implied. Known anchor customers include Nippon Paint, Skshu, Davco (dry mortar/paint), Oriental Yuhong (waterproofing), Wacker (chemicals), Cargill, FrieslandCampina, Olam, Nestlé (food/dairy). (H)
- Intellectual property: 94 patents (company "About us"). (H)

### 3.2 Plastic packaging
- Industrial: **plastic valve bags, heavy-duty FFS film, plastic open-mouth bags, LLDPE and cold-stretch/stretch-hood films**. (H)
- Food and consumer: **multi-layer co-extruded high-barrier films (EVOH)**; **PP/PS cast sheet** for thermoforming (cups, trays); laminated films; **cheese-stick high-barrier film**, where AILU broke an import monopoly in 2018 and claims the No. 1 market share in China. Customers include Milkground (妙可蓝多), Yili and Mengniu. Developing chocolate, confectionery and yogurt packaging next. (H) [Bigdata.com plant visit](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/187CF347F2D6D8D6EE7F6EA73A31E62F)
- **Paper-based plastic-free packaging, "ESpeed® / Speed™ / ESpeed Aishield"**: barrier coating replaces aluminium-plastic laminate. Co-developed with **Mondelez** for Mini Oreo and Tropicana biscuits. Targeting pet-food primary packaging and overseas brand owners with ESG targets. It costs more than plastic laminates and runs on existing paper-sack lines. (H) [ailupack ESpeed Aishield, May 2026](https://www.ailupack.com/espeed-aishield-high-barrier-plastic-film-free-paper-based-packaging/)
- Injection moulding and tubes: via IKKO (§5).

### 3.3 Certifications and quality
- **ISO 9001:2015, ISO 22000:2018, HACCP, BRCGS Packaging Materials** (company site, 2026). Also "food-contact safety compliance, restricted-substance testing (e.g. PFAS), performance validation" and supplier audits for paper, film and pallets. (H) [ailupack About](https://www.ailupack.com/about-ailu-group/); [global-compliance article](https://www.ailupack.com/the-second-half-of-global-compliance-how-ailu-charts-a-durable-growth-curve-for-a-manufacturing-brand-going-global/); [quality article](https://www.ailupack.com/smartquality-control-forglobal-packaging/)

- AILU is a UN Global Compact participant. (M) [UNGC](https://unglobalcompact.org/what-is-gc/participants/196380-Shanghai-Ailu-Package-Co-Ltd-)

### 3.4 Intelligent packing systems: **REVOPAC = AILU's machinery subsidiary (answers the showroom question)**
- **Revopac Packaging Technology Co., Ltd. ("REVOPAC", 上海锐派包装)**, Shanghai, was **acquired by Shanghai Ailu in 2015** and is a **wholly owned subsidiary**. It designs and makes robotic packaging and palletising systems and supplies complete lines: material handling/conveying, weighing, filling, automatic bag placing, palletising and stretch-hooding. (H) [ailupack filling-line page](https://www.ailupack.com/filling-line/); [packaging machinery page](https://www.ailupack.com/packaging-machinery-for-filling/); [Jiemian/Bigdata.com: "wholly-owned subsidiary Ruipai Packaging"](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/DC7B2281C93D4BFB4A60BCA151B70599)
- Products named: "Revopac Filling Family", valve-bag packing machines, robotic bag placer, **automatic valve-bag packing line (negative-pressure type)**, semi-automatic FIBC (big-bag) line (vertical screw type). Industries: chemicals, food, building materials, metallurgy, minerals, new energy. (H)
- 46 sets/lines of intelligent packaging equipment capacity per year (H; Nov 2026 visit article). Revenue H1 2026 RMB 67.8m (+274%), GM 17.15% (H).
- **Commercial angle for Ulusoy:** sacks plus filling line ("bag + machine") bundles; the valve-bag filler and palletiser models seen in the showroom are REVOPAC products.
