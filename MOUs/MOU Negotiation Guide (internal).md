# MOU Negotiation Guide — CRRC, TBEA, AIMS, AILU (Ulusoy internal)

**Do not share with the counterparties.** The four MOUs are drafted in Ulusoy's favour. This guide explains each protective clause, what the other side will probably push back on, and what we can concede without losing the core protection.

All four MOUs share the same structure (16 clauses + Annex A registered opportunities + Annex B registration-notice template). Signing entity: **Ulusoy Robotik ve Teknolojileri A.Ş.** (change if another group company should sign).

---

## 1. The clauses that protect Ulusoy (all binding)

| Clause | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **4.2 Right of first refusal** | Before appointing any other agent, distributor or partner in Türkiye, they must offer it to Ulusoy first, on the same terms | Stops them from quietly appointing a competitor after we open the market |
| **5 Registration** | We register a customer by email; **if they don't reply in 10 working days, it's accepted**; rejection only with dated written proof of prior active talks | Puts the burden of proof on them; no "we already knew them" excuses |
| **5.3 Scope of a registration** | Covers the customer **and its affiliates**, all plants and projects in the Territory | One registration protects a whole group (e.g. a holding with several plants) |
| **5.4 Protection period** | 24 months, **auto-renewing** while talks or orders continue | A long sales cycle (6–10 months for AIMS) doesn't expire our protection |
| **5.5 + Annex A** | Projects already presented are registered **from signing** | Protects what we have already introduced (TBEA/CRRC projects, AIMS's first lead) |
| **6 Non-circumvention** | No direct or indirect dealing with registered customers (incl. affiliates, staff, other partners); enquiries passed to us in 5 days | The core protection |
| **7.1 Scope of remuneration** | Commission on **every** contract, follow-on order, expansion, spare part and service — even if Ulusoy doesn't sign | Captures after-sales value (where AIMS says most value is) |
| **7.2 Floor rate** | Not less than **[●]%** unless agreed otherwise | Fill in before sending (see section 3) |
| **7.3 Payment** | USD, pro-rata within 15 days of each customer payment, **no set-off** | Cash flow tied to theirs, no deductions |
| **7.4 Information rights** | They tell us about every offer, contract, payment; invoice copies on request | Lets us check commissions |
| **7.5 Tail period** | Commission owed on contracts signed **up to 24 months after the MOU ends** | Blocks "terminate, then deal directly" |
| **7.6 Breach** | Full commission **plus liquidated damages [●]%** | A real deterrent |
| **10 Confidentiality** | 5 years; customer information **remains Ulusoy's** | Our leads stay ours |
| **12.2 Non-solicitation** | They can't hire our staff for 12 months after | Protects our team |
| **14 Term** | 3 years, auto-renewing; protection clauses survive | Long protection by default |
| **15 Law and arbitration** | Hong Kong law, HKIAC arbitration, English | Enforceable in mainland China under the HK–mainland arrangement |

## 4. Expected pushback and fallback positions

| Clause | They will likely say | Our fallback (in order) |
|---|---|---|
| Deemed acceptance in 10 days | "Too short" | 15 → 20 working days; keep deemed acceptance |
| 24-month protection, auto-renewing | "Too long" | 18 months + renewal while active → 12 months + renewal |
| Affiliates covered | "Only the named plant" | Named group companies listed in the notice |
| Commission on all orders incl. service | "Only the first contract" | First contract + follow-on orders within 3 years → first contract + spares |
| 24-month tail | "Too long" | 18 → 12 months |
| Liquidated damages | "Not acceptable" | Remove LDs but keep "full commission payable" on breach |
| Binding remuneration in an MOU | "MOU should be non-binding" | Keep 5, 6, 7.5 and 10 binding; move the rate (7.2) to the definitive agreement |
| Right of first refusal | "We can't restrict appointments" | Right of first **negotiation** (they must talk to us first) |
| AIMS implementation role | "We choose our EPC partners" | Ulusoy offered the role on comparable terms ("right to match") |
| AILU distributor ROFR | "Too early" | ROFR limited to product lines Ulusoy has registered customers for |
| HK arbitration | "Chinese law / CIETAC" | Singapore (SIAC) → CIETAC Beijing in English as last resort |

**Never concede:** registration itself, non-circumvention, confidentiality of our customer information, and some tail period. Without these the MOU gives us nothing.

## 5. Process tips

1. **Register before naming.** Send the MOU with Annex A filled in; for new leads, always send a registration email first and keep the acknowledgement.
2. **Send the DOCX for comments, the PDF for reading.** Ask them to mark up changes in the Word file.
3. **Let them propose their template if they insist,** but bring our clauses 5–7 into it.
4. **Have a Turkish lawyer (ideally with China experience) review** before signature.
5. **Keep practical protection too:** stay on all customer communications, and where possible sign an NDA directly between Ulusoy and the customer.
